EasyCRM

Phone prospecting & call-center for Odoo

Compatible with Odoo 18.0 — Community and Enterprise. Standalone: depends only on base and mail. Does not modify Odoo's CRM app.

Features

  • Prospect lists — Contacts with normalized phone numbers (Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia, France or any country), category, region, assigned salesperson, status and callback date. Duplicates are detected.
  • One-click call logging — Log the outcome in a pop-up, add a note and a callback date, then jump to the next prospect with Save & next or the Call next menu (due callbacks first).
  • Callbacks that do not get lost — A native Odoo activity is scheduled for every callback, plus a calendar view and a Callbacks due filter.
  • Configurable call outcomes — Define your own responses: stays in the call queue or not, callback required, note required, automatic retry after N hours, counts as customer or refusal.
  • Custom fields — Add text, number, date, yes/no, single-choice and multiple-choice fields. They are real Odoo fields: filters, group-by, export and native import all work.
  • Teams & categories — Each team manages product categories. A salesperson sees his own prospects plus the unassigned pool of his team.
  • Excel / CSV import — Columns are recognized by name; duplicates (database and file) are skipped; phone numbers and Algerian wilayas (French or Arabic) are cleaned automatically.
  • Fair distribution — Distribute unassigned prospects round-robin between salespeople by category, or on any selection from the list.
  • Statistics — Calls by day, hour, outcome, salesperson and category; prospects by category and status; Excel export from every pivot.
  • WhatsApp — One button opens a WhatsApp conversation with a greeting message.
  • Roles — Administrator, Supervisor, Salesperson, Read-only — enforced with Odoo groups and record rules.

Installation

Install the module from the Apps menu, then assign the EasyCRM roles to your users in Settings → Users. The user interface is in French.
